Voice Shortcuts
Android · installable web app
Your voice, your choice

Say what you want to open.

Use short voice commands to open Instagram or Snapchat. You stay in control of messages and stories.

Try “open Instagram” or “draft message to name saying hello”.
Try these commands
“Open Instagram” or “Open Snapchat”
“Draft message to [name] saying [your message]”
“Prepare a story mention [username]”
Privacy and control

Tap the microphone each time; this app does not listen in the background. Your browser or Android speech service recognizes speech and may use an online provider. This app does not save audio or command history.

Messages and story posts are never sent by this shortcut app. Review and complete them in Instagram or Snapchat yourself.

Opening a link may switch to the installed social app when Android recognizes it; otherwise it opens the website. This app does not read your social messages or contacts.
